Product origin & history

The quarter-zip pullover originated in 1970s athletic and military performance wear, designed for easy on/off temperature regulation during training. It crossed into mainstream casualwear through golf and ski culture in the 1990s, popularized by brands like Patagonia and Polo Ralph Lauren. In 2024–2026 it was fully adopted by high fashion after Miu Miu, Chanel, and Tory Burch featured it on their runways, transforming it from a sportswear staple into a fashion-forward everyday item.
